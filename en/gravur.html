What do you need for a quote?

A product idea, quantity, approximate dimensions and a logo or artwork help us prepare an estimate. We can also advise you if your idea is still taking shape.

Yes. We discuss suitable materials and the finish, and agree the design before production.

Can I collect my order or arrange delivery?

Collection in Berlin or delivery is arranged for each project. We clarify the process and costs in your quote.
