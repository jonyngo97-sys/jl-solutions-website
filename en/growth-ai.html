Getting started, explained.

Can I review and edit content myself?

Yes. You can edit drafts and media and prepare content to suit your restaurant.

Are posts published automatically?

Scheduled posts can be transferred automatically for supported accounts and formats when setup is active and content is complete. Without a suitable connection, drafts and manual exports remain available. We agree the usable scope during setup.

Can I reply to every message directly?

The inbox shows content returned by your connected account. External replies depend on platform permissions, the allowed reply window and separate publishing approval. You can review reply drafts first.
