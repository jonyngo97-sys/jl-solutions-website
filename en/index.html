Any questions?

Do my guests need an app?

No. The Smart QR restaurant page opens in a browser after scanning. The chat on this website also works without a WhatsApp account.

Can I update content myself?

Yes. Your customer account lets you manage the enabled areas for your restaurant, such as your menu, design and marketing drafts.

Is every social feature available immediately?

The scope depends on connected accounts, platform permissions and configuration. Before you start, we agree which analytics, formats and publishing features are available for your business.
